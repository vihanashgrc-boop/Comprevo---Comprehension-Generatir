Bloom's Taxonomy Reading Skills Framework

Comprevo passages and question sets are mapped against recognized educational standards to evaluate every level of reading proficiency:

  • Remembering & Retrieval: Identifying direct facts, setting details, dates, and explicit textual evidence.
  • Understanding & Paraphrasing: Summarizing central ideas, theme recognition, and main argument synthesis.
  • Applying & Inferencing: Drawing logical deductions from subtle contextual hints and character motives.
  • Analyzing & Evaluating: Deconstructing author tone, rhetorical techniques, bias, and passage structure.
  • Vocabulary in Context: Decoding unfamiliar idioms, synonyms, antonyms, and morphological roots.
